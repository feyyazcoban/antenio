Privacy policy

Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE] · App: Antenio (Android TV and Google TV)

In short: Antenio needs no account, uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and sends nothing about you to us or to third parties. The sources you add and your settings stay on your TV.

Who is responsible

Antenio is developed and published by [NAME / COMPANY, ADDRESS]. For any privacy question: destek@antenio.app

Data we collect

As the developer of Antenio, we collect no personal data from you. The app has no account or sign-up, and no analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, advertising or crash-reporting libraries.

What stays on your TV

  • Your sources: the M3U link or server address, username and password you add. Stored encrypted with an Android Keystore key (AES-GCM).
  • Catalog cache: the channel, movie and series lists from your source. Stored encrypted.
  • PIN and lock settings: stored encrypted.
  • Favorites, watch history and resume points: stored unencrypted in storage only the app can read.

None of this goes into cloud backup or device transfer, and none of it leaves the TV.

Where the app connects

Antenio connects only to the source and guide addresses you add and to the stream and image addresses those sources list. As with any internet connection, the provider's server sees your TV's IP address and the app's identifier (the "Antenio" User-Agent). How the provider handles that is governed by its own policy; we do not run those servers.

Adding from your phone

While the "Add with your phone" screen is open, the TV serves a small setup page on your home network. What you enter on your phone goes only to the TV inside your home network, never through our servers or anyone else's. The page closes with the screen.

Deleting your data

  • A source and its history: Settings → Sources → Remove source.
  • All sources, favorites, history and the PIN: on the PIN screen, "Forgot PIN" → "Reset everything".
  • Everything: uninstall the app from the TV.

Children

Antenio is not directed at children. A parental PIN can lock categories, channels and sources, and the adult content filter is on by default.

Google Play

Downloading the app from Google Play and any payments are governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. Google Play may show aggregated crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) for the app; these depend on your Google settings.

Changes

If the app introduces new data processing (for example an account or a subscription), this page is updated beforehand and its date changes.
